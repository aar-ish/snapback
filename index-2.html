Why SnapBack

Built for the way you
actually lose focus

No bloat. No permissions. No subscription. Just a tiny app in your menu bar doing one thing extremely well.

🔕
Zero permissions

Uses IOKit's HIDIdleTime to read system idle state — no accessibility permissions, no screen recording, no surveillance.

⏸
Smart pause

Automatically silences alerts when your screen is locked or media is playing. No false alarms during a movie or presentation.

🎙
Personality sounds

Choose from system sounds, TTS personalities like "Disappointed Parent", or upload your own custom audio file.

🔁
Repeat alerts

If you're still away, SnapBack will remind you again. Configurable repeat interval so it doesn't give up on you.

📊
Daily idle stats

Tracks your alert count and idle time per day. See your patterns and figure out when you drift the most.

🚀
Launch at login

Register with SMAppService so SnapBack is always running when you sit down. Set it, forget it.

How it works

Three steps.
No setup drama.

01
Install & run

Drop it in your Applications folder. It hides in the menu bar and never touches the Dock again.

02
Set your threshold

Pick how many minutes of silence before SnapBack speaks up. 1 min, 5 mins, or a custom value up to 2 hours.

03
Pick your voice

Choose a system chime, a passive-aggressive TTS phrase, or drop in your own audio file. Your call.

04
Get back to work

When the alert fires, you're already snapping back. That's it. The whole app.
